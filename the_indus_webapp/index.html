  • Seasonal Trends: The river's flow exhibits a distinct seasonal pattern, with a significant discharge peak every summer and much lower flow during other times of the year.

  • Baseline Model Performance: The Baseline Model captures the general seasonal cycle but is often inaccurate, struggling to correctly predict the magnitude of peak flows and appearing unstable during low-flow periods.

  • Hybrid AI Model Performance: The Hybrid AI Model tracks the actual discharge with much greater accuracy, proving superior at predicting both the timing and size of peak flows while remaining more stable year-round.
  • Autocorrelation: No statistically significant autocorrelation at any lag 1–12 months.

  • Durbin-Watson: 1.751 (baseline), 2.195 (hybrid).
  • Model performance: The model performs well in the upper Indus Basin(Blue region), where streamflow is primarily driven by snow and glacier melt captured through satellite data.
  • Downstream reliability decreases: Limited access to ground discharge data for calibration and increasing human intervention (reservoir operations, diversions) are not explicitly modeled.
  • Implication: Predictions beyond major control points such as Tarbela(Red region) should be interpreted with caution.
  • Glacier dynamics not explicitly modeled — melt proxy is a simplification .
